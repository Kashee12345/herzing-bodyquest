A pharmacology book written for nursing — every drug class in plain English, anchored to what actually matters at the bedside: what it does, what to watch for, and what to teach. Real figures, comparison tables, clinical pearls, and a self-check in every chapter.

by Dr. Akash Garg · NSG 124 · Herzing University
💊 A note: the book runs 14 chapters — the principles, then a tour of every major drug class by body system. Each chapter explains the drugs plainly, shows a real figure, and ends with a self-check. Written to work for any nursing program.
