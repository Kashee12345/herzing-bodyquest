INTERVENTIONS · DIALS

FREE WATER (D5W)OFF
Lowers the sodium. Your tool to RE-LOWER if the correction has gone too fast.

Freezes water handling to STOP the sodium moving — the brake when a correction is running away.

CASE DEBRIEF

SODIUM vs THE SAFE CORRECTION CORRIDOR

The blue line is the true sodium; the dots are the sodiums you actually drew. The dashed line is the safe ceiling — about 8 mEq of rise per 24 hours. Stay under it and the brain is safe. Push the line into the red zone above it, even briefly, and you injure the myelin.

CORRECTION RATE — THE THING THAT ACTUALLY MATTERS

The same story as a speed. The gold line is your projected 24-hour rate of rise. As long as it stays under the green line (~8 mEq/24h) you are safe; above the red line (~12) demyelination risk accrues fast. This is what you were titrating, whether you knew it or not.

correction rate (mEq/24h) safe limit rapid limit

TWO OPPOSING DANGERS — SWELLING NOW vs DEMYELINATION LATER

Cerebral edema (blue) is the acute danger of a sodium that is too LOW — it falls as you raise the sodium. Demyelination risk (pink) is the hidden, irreversible danger of raising it too FAST. It only ever goes up. The art is relieving the first without triggering the second.
