INTERVENTIONS · DIALS

OXYGEN (FiO₂)OFF
Raises the saturation — aim for a target of 88-92%, no higher. Too much suppresses his drive to breathe; too little starves him.
BRONCHODILATORS (NEBS)OFF
Open the airways, ease the work of breathing, and improve gas exchange. Core treatment of the exacerbation.
STEROIDSOFF
Reduce airway inflammation over hours — a slower but important part of treating the flare.
NON-INVASIVE VENT (BiPAP) OFF
Mechanically blows off CO₂ and unloads the tiring muscles — the escalation when ventilation, not oxygen, is failing.
