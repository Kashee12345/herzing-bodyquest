INTERVENTIONS · DIALS

IV CRYSTALLOIDOFF
Raises volume fast, but carries no oxygen and dilutes hemoglobin and clotting factors. Useful early, dangerous as a crutch.
BLOOD PRODUCTSOFF
Refills volume AND restores oxygen carriage and clotting. The real resuscitation in hemorrhage.
SOURCE CONTROLOFF
Pressure now, definitive control over time. Without it you are filling a leaking tank.
VASOPRESSORSOFF
Squeezes the vessels to raise the pressure reading — but it cannot fill an empty tank, and it worsens tissue perfusion.

HEMOGLOBIN & CLOTTING

Hemoglobin lags — it can look near-normal right after acute loss. Crystalloid-heavy stretches (shaded) dilute both the hemoglobin and the hidden clotting reserve, so the blood thins exactly when it needs to clot.
