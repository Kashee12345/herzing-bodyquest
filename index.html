Guide to the Tools
🎓

Exam Prep HQ in-hub

Your exam command centre. A timed and scored practice exam drawn from the 365-question BodyQuest bank, plus rapid drills for value signatures, easily-confused look-alike pairs, mechanism chains, and telling normal physiology from pathology — with an ABG trainer and a printable cram sheet. A readiness dashboard tracks your accuracy unit by unit and names the one to study first.

📝

Study Guides view & print

A complete written review for each unit, with topic navigators, exam cheat sheets and glossaries. Every guide has a Print / Save PDF button that expands all sections first, so you get the whole thing rather than just the headings.

🔬

The A&P Lab in-hub

Hands-on animated lessons — click, drag and toggle to watch a mechanism actually happen. The fastest way to make a process click when the text isn't landing.

🧍

3D Anatomy Atlas in-hub

118 explorable 3D models. Rotate and zoom real anatomy instead of squinting at a flat diagram — ideal for learning spatial relationships before a practical.

🃏

Flashcards in-hub

Flip decks for every unit with a daily challenge that keeps a streak. The fastest way to drill terms and structures.

🎮

The Games in-hub

BodyQuest is a pixel-art quest where each body system is a zone with a boss; the Quiz Show is a race against the clock; PhysiologyQuest and CoronaryQuest 3D go deeper on function. All teach with full explanations after every answer.

📖

The A&P Book in-hub

An interactive textbook for the course, with figures, tables and the lab animations embedded where they belong.

📈 A simple way to study each unit

  1. Meet the concepts in The Book or the unit's Study Guide.
  2. Watch the mechanism move in The A&P Lab.
  3. See the structures in 3D in the Atlas.
  4. Drill the terms with the Flashcards.
  5. Lock it in by playing BodyQuest and the Quiz Show.

🎓 When an exam is coming

  1. Open Exam Prep HQ and run the practice exam cold, in All Units mode.
  2. Read the readiness dashboard — it names your weakest unit. Start there.
  3. Re-read that unit's study guide, then drill it: value signatures, look-alikes, mechanism chains.
  4. Re-run the exam and watch the weak units come up.
  5. The night before, read the Cram Sheet.
